## Browse. Queue. Save.

- Browse and search Apple Music inside the app.
- Sign in on Apple's page in the embedded browser.
- Add individual tracks, albums or playlists to your download queue.
- Keep original AAC/M4A audio or choose MP3 output.
- Save artwork and metadata, with optional lyrics.
- Check per-track results and retry failed tracks.
- Review your download history.
- Choose your destination folder and open it in Finder.

No terminal setup, Safari extension, pairing code or cookies-file export is needed for ordinary use.

## Requirements

- macOS 14 or later.
- An Apple Silicon or Intel Mac.
- Internet access and an Apple Music account with an active subscription for subscription-catalogue downloads.

.pod. is free. Apple Music is a separate service; .pod. does not include an Apple Music subscription.

## Install

1. Download the DMG matching your Mac.
2. Open the DMG and drag **pod.app** into **Applications**.
3. Open **pod** from Applications.
4. Sign in to Apple Music inside the app when required.
5. Browse or paste an Apple Music link, queue your music and choose where to save it.

The default destination is **~/Music/pod Downloads**. You can change it in the app.

**Signing:** this release is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarised. macOS may require approval in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** after an initial launch attempt.

## Bringing music to an iPod

.pod. began when AL, its creator at Maya@Sea, dusted off his iPod Classic and connected it to his Mac. He built .pod. to bring back the desktop music workflow he wanted.

1. **Save audio locally with .pod.** Browse Apple Music, queue your selection and save supported tracks to your Mac.
2. **Import the saved file into Music.** Add compatible saved files as local audio.
3. **Sync the local file to your iPod Classic using Music.** Music performs the device sync.

.pod. handles browsing, queueing and local saving. It does not manage or sync the iPod itself.
